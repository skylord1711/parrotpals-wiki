Crates are the loot system on Parrot Pals. There are **six crates**, each with its own loot table, price, and animation.

## The six crates

| Crate | Cost | Animation | Best loot |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [End Crate](/crates/end-crate/) | 10,000 Vault | Enclosing | Elytra, Dragon Egg, Dragon Head |
| [Vote Crate](/crates/vote-crate/) | Free (voting, 1 limit) | Roulette | Elytra (end-themed) |
| [Animal Crate](/crates/animal-crate/) | 4,000 Vault | Mystery | Rare animals, mythic pets |
| [Hostile Mob Crate](/crates/hostile-mob-crate/) | 6,000 Vault | Storm | Warden, Wither Skeleton, boss mobs |
| [Nametag Crate](/crates/nametag-crate/) | 6,000 Vault | Roulette | Exclusive nametag prefixes |
| [Pet Crate](/crates/pets-crate/) | 10,000 Vault | Roulette | Every pet on the server |

## Rarity tiers

Every crate has loot split into tiers:

- **Common** — everyday rewards
- **Rare** — solid upgrades and mid-tier loot
- **Mythic** — the jackpot. Mythic wins are **broadcast to the server**!

## Drop chances

Each opening rolls a **rarity by weight** (**Common 70, Rare 25, Mythic 5** — normalized only over the rarities present in that crate), then a **reward by weight** inside that rarity. See the full per-reward percentages on each crate page:

| Crate | Common | Rare | Mythic |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [End Crate](/crates/end-crate/) | 70% | 25% | 5% |
| [Vote Crate](/crates/vote-crate/) | 93.33% | — | 6.67% |
| [Animal Crate](/crates/animal-crate/) | 70% | 25% | 5% |
| [Hostile Mob Crate](/crates/hostile-mob-crate/) | 70% | 25% | 5% |
| [Nametag Crate](/crates/nametag-crate/) | 70% | 25% | 5% |
| [Pet Crate](/crates/pets-crate/) | 70% | 25% | 5% |

The **Vote Crate** has no Rare tier, so its Common share jumps to 93.33%. Within the **Pet Crate**, **Parrot King** is the rarest reward on the server at **0.556%**.

## How to open

The crates live around **spawn** as NPCs (plus physical blocks you can interact with):

1. **Left-click** a crate NPC to **preview** its loot table (paged, grouped by rarity).
2. **Right-click** a crate NPC to **open** it.
3. Or use `/crates` / `/crates menu` to browse every crate.
4. Pay the crate cost and watch the animation roll your loot.

Previewing is free — only opening costs money, so check the loot before you pay.

## Getting keys

- **Vote Crate** — vote for the server (see [Vote Crate](/crates/vote-crate/)).
- **Money crates** — pay with Vault money each opening.
- The **Lucky Miner** pickaxe upgrade and prestige rewards can grant extra keys/rewards.
