Crates are the loot system on Parrot Pals. There are **five crates**, each with its own loot table, price, and animation.

## The five crates

| Crate | Cost | Animation | Best loot |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [End Crate](/crates/end-crate/) | 5,000 Vault | Enclosing | Elytra, Dragon Egg, Dragon Head |
| [Vote Crate](/crates/vote-crate/) | Free (voting, 1 limit) | Roulette | $15,000, Netherite gear |
| [Animal Crate](/crates/animal-crate/) | 1,500 Vault | Mystery | Rare animals, mythic pets |
| [Hostile Mob Crate](/crates/hostile-mob-crate/) | 3,500 Vault | Storm | Warden, Wither Skeleton, boss mobs |
| [Nametag Crate](/crates/nametag-crate/) | 5,000 Vault | Roulette | Exclusive nametag prefixes |

## Rarity tiers

Every crate has loot split into tiers:

- **Common** — everyday rewards
- **Rare** — solid upgrades and mid-tier loot
- **Mythic** — the jackpot. Mythic wins are **broadcast to the server**!

## How to open

1. Right-click a placed crate (they're located around spawn).
2. Or open the crate menu with `/crates` / `/crates menu`.
3. Pay the crate cost and watch the animation roll your loot.

## Getting keys

- **Vote Crate** — vote for the server (see [Vote Crate](/crates/vote-crate/)).
- **Money crates** — pay with Vault money each opening.
- The **Lucky Miner** pickaxe upgrade and prestige rewards can grant extra keys/rewards.
