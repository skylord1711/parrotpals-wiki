## Drop chances

Every roll first picks a rarity by weight (**Common 70, Rare 25, Mythic 5**), then picks a reward inside that rarity. With 14 rewards total:

| Rarity | Chance | Per reward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Common (5 rewards) | 70% | **14.00%** each |
| Rare (5 rewards) | 25% | **5.00%** each |
| Mythic (4 rewards) | 5% | **1.25%** each |

## Notes

- Mythic drops are announced in chat — everyone will know you hit the jackpot!
- No opening limit — grind it as long as you can afford it.
- The **Elytra** and **Dragon Egg** are the rarest, most valuable items on the crate.
