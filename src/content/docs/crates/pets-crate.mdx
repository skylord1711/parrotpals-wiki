## Drop chances

Every roll first picks a rarity by weight (**Common 70, Rare 25, Mythic 5**), then picks a reward inside that rarity. With 39 rewards total:

| Rarity | Chance | Per reward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Common (17 rewards) | 70% | **4.118%** each |
| Rare (17 rewards) | 25% | **1.471%** each |
| Mythic (5 rewards) | 5% | Galaxy Parrot, Unicorn, Crystal Dragon, Red Dragon: **1.111%** each |

Within Mythic, the **Parrot King** uses a lower weight (1 vs. 2), so it sits at just **0.556%** — the rarest reward on the server.

## Notes

- Pets are granted instantly on win — no coupon to claim.
- The **Parrot King** is the rarest reward in the crate.
- Mythic pets are broadcast to the whole server.
- See the full pet list on the [Pets page](/progression/pets/).
