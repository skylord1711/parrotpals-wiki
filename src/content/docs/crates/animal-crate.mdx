## Drop chances

Every roll first picks a rarity by weight (**Common 70, Rare 25, Mythic 5**), then picks a reward inside that rarity. With 38 rewards total:

| Rarity | Chance | Per reward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Common (12 rewards) | 70% | **5.83%** each |
| Rare (16 rewards) | 25% | **1.56%** each |
| Mythic (10 rewards) | 5% | **0.50%** each |

## Notes

- Great for filling your farm or zoo on the SMP.
- Mythic rewards like the **Sniffer**, **Camel**, and **Armadillo** are broadcast.
- No opening limit.
