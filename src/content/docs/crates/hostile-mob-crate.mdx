## Drop chances

Every roll first picks a rarity by weight (**Common 70, Rare 25, Mythic 5**), then picks a reward inside that rarity. With 36 rewards total:

| Rarity | Chance | Per reward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Common (12 rewards) | 70% | **5.83%** each |
| Rare (10 rewards) | 25% | **2.50%** each |
| Mythic (14 rewards) | 5% | **0.357%** each |

## Notes

- A **Warden** or **Elder Guardian** spawner egg here is a massive win — those mobs pay 2,000–5,000 🪙 and 50–100 🪙 per kill.
- Mythic drops are broadcast.
