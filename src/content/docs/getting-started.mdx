Welcome to Parrot Pals! Here's everything you need to know to get started.

## Join the server

| Edition | Address | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Java | `parrotpals.net` | Version 26.2 (Paper), crossplay enabled |
| Bedrock | `parrotpals.net` | Port `25615`, crossplay enabled |

The server supports **Java and Bedrock crossplay**, so you can play no matter what you're on.

## First join checklist

1. **Join the Discord** — [discord.gg/parrotpals](https://discord.gg/parrotpals) to stay updated on events, giveaways, and streams.
2. **Claim your Starter Kit** — every player gets it once (`/kit` → Starter Kit).
3. **Spawn in the Prison** — start mining in the **Budgie** mine, your first rank is free.
4. **Open your first crates** — free keys and opening for the [Vote Crate](/crates/vote-crate/) (free, 1 limit), plus [Animal](/crates/animal-crate/) and [Hostile Mob](/crates/hostile-mob-crate/) crates for starters.
5. **Check the vote rewards** — vote for the server to earn the [Vote Crate](/crates/vote-crate/), plus **Voting Points** to spend in the [Vote Shop](/crates/vote-crate/#vote-shop).
6. **Spend your ultcoins** — buy spawners in the [Spawner Shop](/economy/spawner-shop/) and enchanted books in the [Quest Shop](/economy/quest-shop/).

## Useful first commands

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/kit` | Open the kits menu |
| `/spawn` | Teleport to spawn |
| `/warp` | View available warps |
| `/balance` or `/bal` | Check your Vault money (the $ used for prison ranks) |
| `/ultcoins` | Check your ultcoin balance 🪙 |
| `/rtp` | Random teleport to the SMP |
| `/votefree` or menu | Open the vote crate |
| `/pvp` | Toggle PvP on/off |

## Three things you'll track

- **Vault money ($)** — earned from selling mined blocks, mob drops, and quests. Used to buy prison ranks and kit items.
- **Tokens** — earned from prison crates, prestige, and mining. Part of the prison progression system.
- **Ultcoins 🪙** — earned from killing hostile mobs and chat games. Spent in the spawner shop and quest shop.
