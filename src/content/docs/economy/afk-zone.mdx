The **AFK Zone** is a water pool at **spawn** (world `afk`) that pays you for simply standing in it. It's the only fully **passive** money on the server.

## The reward

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Reward | **$15** every **60 seconds** |
| Rate | **$900 / hour** |
| Cooldown after reward | None — it keeps paying every minute |
| Requirement | Just stand inside the pool |

You earn $15 every minute for as long as you stay in the zone. There's no anti-AFK gimmick and no daily cap — you can leave your character in the pool and come back to a bigger balance.

## How it works

1. Walk into the pool — you get the message *"You have entered the AFK zone! You will get a reward in 60s!"*
2. A **bossbar** shows your **total AFK time** and time until the next reward.
3. Every 60 seconds you get a **$15 ParrotPals** payout.
4. Leave any time — you'll see your total time stayed and total rewards earned.

Your **lifetime AFK time** is tracked permanently (even after leaving), which powers the leaderboard.

## AFK Leaderboard 🏆

A hologram next to the pool ranks players by **total time spent in the AFK zone** — not by money. The board is built with the `%frostyafkzone_total_seconds%` placeholder, so it shows lifetime AFK time even when players are offline.

At $900/hr the AFK Zone pays about **10% of a good active job** — it's a low-but-real passive trickle, not a way to out-earn active players. Every single job on the server pays more. It's the perfect money for when you're away from the keyboard, not the path to the top of [baltop](/economy/overview/).
