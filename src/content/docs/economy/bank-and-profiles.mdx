## Bank 🏦

Keep your ultcoins safe in the bank so you never lose your wallet on death.

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Max bank balance | 10,000,000 🪙 |
| Withdrawal fee | None (0%) |

- **Deposit:** `/bank deposit <amount>`
- **Withdraw:** `/bank withdraw <amount>`

## Baltop 📊

View the top **10 richest players** on the server.

- Open with `/baltop`
- Refreshes every 5 minutes

## Profiles 👤

Every player has a profile card showing their balances and stats.

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/profile` | View your own profile |
| `/profile <player>` | View another player's profile |

Player profiles are **publicly viewable**, so feel free to flex your ultcoin balance.
