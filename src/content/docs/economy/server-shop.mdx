The **server shop** is where you buy **building materials and everyday items** with your Vault balance. It carries **900+ items across 15 categories** (building blocks, farming, storage, natural blocks, ocean, end, and more). Open it with **`/shop`** or **`/market`**.

## Buying & selling

Every item in the shop has **two prices**: a **buy price** (pay Vault money to get the item) and a **sell price** (sell your items back to the shop for money).

- **Sell price is always 25% of the buy price** (a flat 4:1 ratio) — so if you buy for $10, you can sell it back for $2.50.
- **Sell your whole inventory** with `/sellall`, or open the sell menu with `/sellgui`.

## The pricey stuff 💎

Only **21 items** cost $500 or more — and they're all intentional. The big three:

| Item | Buy | Sell |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Dragon Egg** | $50,000 | Cannot sell |
| **Shulker Box** (all 17 colors) | $624 | $156 |
| **Sponge** | $525 | $131.25 |

The **Dragon Egg** is the single most expensive item on the server — the ultimate flex and a match for the [End Crate](/crates/end-crate/) rewards. Shulkers are a premium storage convenience, and sponges are a handy ocean-building tool.

## Fairness

Prices line up with the economy: even the weakest earner (the [AFK Zone](/economy/afk-zone/) at $900/hr) can afford basic materials within minutes, while the crates' 4k–16k price tags match a few hours of active [jobs](/economy/jobs/) play. The expensive items are meant to stay aspirational, not impossible.
