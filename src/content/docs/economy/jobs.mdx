Jobs pay you **money** for doing activities. You can have **2 jobs active at once**. Open the jobs menu with `/jobs`, `/job`, or `/jobsmenu`.

## Fisherman 🎣

Earn money by **fishing** — $5.00 per catch.

## Crafter 🔨

Earn money by **crafting items**.

| Item | Pay |
| --- | --- |
| Diamond Sword / Pickaxe / Axe | $10.00 |
| Diamond Shovel / Hoe | $8.00 |
| Iron Sword / Pickaxe / Axe | $3.00 |
| Golden Apple | $15.00 |
| Enchanted Golden Apple | $100.00 |
| Cake | $5.00 |
| Bread | $1.00 |
| Cookie | $0.50 |
| Quartz Block | $2.00 |
| Glowstone | $3.00 |

## Tips

- Job money goes to your **Vault balance**.
- Pair **Miner** with prison grinding — you get job pay *and* sell the blocks.
- **Crafter** pays big for Enchanted Golden Apples ($100) and diamond gear ($10).
