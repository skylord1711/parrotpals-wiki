Jobs pay you **money** for doing activities. You can have **3 jobs active at once**. Open the jobs menu with `/jobs`, `/job`, or `/jobsmenu`.

Every amount below is the **base pay at level 1**. Your pay **scales up +1% per job level** — and **down −5% per extra job** you own beyond the first (so running 3 jobs means −10%).

## Fairness overview

| Job | Est. $/hr | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Builder 🔨 | $8,000–15,000 | **Strongest $/effort** — placing is fast and free. |
| Hunter 🏹 | $5,000–12,000 | **Best combat earner**, intended top tier. |
| Farmer 🌾 | $6,000–9,000 | Solid. |
| Miner ⛏️ | $4,000–8,000 | Solid, well anti-cheated. |
| Woodcutter 🪵 | $3,600–7,000 | Fair. |
| Fisherman 🎣 | $3,000–5,000 | Fair. |
| Crafter / Enchanter / Weaponsmith | $2,000–5,000 | Fair (material/XP-gated). |
| Explorer 🧭 | $2,000–3,000 | Fair. |
| Brewer ⚗️ | $1,000–2,500 | Fair (slow by design). |
| Digger ⛏️ | $1,500–3,000 | Fair as the starter job. |

**Bottom line:** every job beats the [AFK Zone](/economy/afk-zone/) ($900/hr). Hunter and Builder are the top earners; Digger, Brewer and Explorer are intentionally the lowest.
