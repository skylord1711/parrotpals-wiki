title: Player Market
description: The auction house, player shops and chest shops — buy and sell items with other players using Vault money.
---

The **player market** is where you buy and sell items **with other players** — instead of the fixed-price [Server Shop](/economy/server-shop/), you set your own prices. Everything runs on Vault money (**$**), and there are **no listing fees**.

It comes in three parts:

| Feature | Command | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Auction House** | `/ah` | Sell to the whole server. Players can bid on your listing. |
| **Player Shops** | `/shops` | Your own shop menu where players browse and buy. |
| **Chest Shops** | shift-right-click a chest | A physical shop block with a hologram. |

## Auction House (`/ah`)

The big marketplace — list an item and any player on the server can buy or bid on it.

- **2 listings** per player at a time.
- Listings last **5 days (120 hours)**.
- **Bidding is on** — buyers can place a bid and the highest bidder wins when the time runs out.
- Buyers buy the **whole listing** (no partial purchases) at the price you set.
- List an item in your hand with **`/listitem`** (alias `/list`).

## Player Shops (`/shops`)

Your personal shop where players can buy from you directly.

- **2 shops** per player, with **5 items listed** in total across them.
- Listings last **5 days (120 hours)**.
- **Restocking is on** — you can top up stock up to **9 stacks** of each item.

## Chest Shops

A physical selling block: place a chest, shift-right-click it to create a shop, set your price, and fill the chest with stock. A **hologram** floats above showing the item, stock and price.

- **2 chest shops** per player.
- Allowed in the **main world** only (disabled in the nether and end).
- Buyers click the chest to buy; stock comes straight out of the chest's inventory.

## The Inbox (`/inbox`)

Everything that comes back to you lands in your **inbox**:

- Items that **expired** without selling.
- Money from **sold** items.
- Auction wins and offers you missed.

You get a message on join whenever you have unclaimed items or incoming offers.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/ah` or `/auction` | Open the auction house |
| `/listitem` or `/list` | List the item in your hand on the auction house |
| `/shops` or `/playershops` | Open your player shops |
| `/inbox` | Claim unclaimed items and money |
