## Money farming tips

- **Spawners** are the best income: buy a spawner, farm it, get ultcoins. See the [Spawner Shop](/economy/spawner-shop/).
- The **Warden** is the jackpot (up to 5,000 🪙) but is extremely dangerous.
- Bosses like the Wither and Ender Dragon pay 500–2,000+ 🪙 per kill.
- Sell unused spawners back for **50%** of what you paid.
