Every way to earn on Parrot Pals, and exactly how much it gives. This is the big-picture version of the [Jobs](/economy/jobs/) page — it covers **all** money and ultcoin sources, not just jobs.

Two currencies are in play:

- **Vault money ($)** — from jobs, AFK, selling prison blocks, bosses, quests, daily rewards and prison crates.
- **Ultcoins 🪙** — from hostile mob kills, chat games, and selling spawners back.

## Active earning (your hourly grind)

| Source | How much | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[Jobs](/economy/jobs/)** | **$2,000–15,000/hr** | 12 jobs, up to 3 active. Builder $8–15k/hr, Hunter $5–12k/hr, Miner $4–8k/hr, down to Digger $1.5–3k/hr. |
| **[Mob kills](/economy/mob-rewards/)** | **1–5,000 🪙 per kill** | Silverfish 1–3 🪙 up to Warden 2,000–5,000 🪙. Passive mobs give nothing. |
| **[Chat games](/community/chat-games/)** | **1,000 🪙 per win** | 5 games run all day, first correct answer wins. |
| **[Prison bosses](/prison/bosses/)** | **$50,000–200,000 + 500–2,000 EXP** | Spawn while mining (~every 1,000 blocks). Warden Prisoner pays the most. |
| **[Selling prison blocks](/prison/sell-prices/)** | **up to $10,800/block** | Stone $50 up to Netherite Block $10,800. Autosell as you mine. |

## Passive earning

| Source | How much | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[AFK Zone](/economy/afk-zone/)** | **$15 / 60 seconds = $900/hr** | Stand in the AFK zone to get paid. Safe, free, but the slowest earner. |

## Daily & weekly

| Source | How much | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[Daily quests](/community/daily-quests/)** | **$100–350 per job per day** | 1 quest set per job per day (up to 3 jobs = up to ~$1,050/day). Exact values on the [Jobs](/economy/jobs/) page. |
| **[Daily rewards](/prison/daily-rewards/)** | **$1,000–5,000 + 500–1,000 tokens + 1–3 gems** | Log in daily, weekly cycle. |
| **[Voting](/crates/vote-crate/)** | **1 Vote Crate per site (up to 3/day) + Vote Points** | Vote on each site once a day. The crate is end-themed (Elytra is the jackpot) — no direct money. |

## Crate payouts (gambling, not income)

| Source | How much | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[Prison crates](/prison/prison-crates/)** | **$500 / $2,500 / $10,000 or tokens** | Common 25% → $500, Uncommon 40% → $2,500, Rare 45% → $10,000 (jackpot 5% → 5,000 tokens). |

## Progression payouts

| Source | How much | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[Prestige](/prison/prestige/)** | **10,000 tokens + permanent +10% income per prestige** | Income bonus stacks and applies to everything you earn. The only money source that multiplies the rest. |

## Ranking the earners

| Source | Est. $/hr | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prison Bosses 🧟 | $0–50k+ (event) | **Biggest single payouts**, but they spawn randomly. |
| Jobs (Builder/Hunter) 🔨🏹 | $8,000–15,000 | **Best consistent active grind.** |
| Jobs (other) | $2,000–8,000 | Good middle ground. |
| AFK Zone 😴 | $900 | Slow but **free** — beats doing nothing. |
| Daily quests 🎯 | up to ~$1,050/day | Small daily bonus. |
| Mob kills (ultcoins) 🪙 | 1–5,000/kill | Feeds the spawner shop, not your $ balance. |

**Bottom line:** play jobs for $, kill mobs for 🪙, and AFK only as a bonus — every active source pays more than the [AFK Zone](/economy/afk-zone/).
