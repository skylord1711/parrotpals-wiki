Parrot Pals runs **two economies**:

1. **Vault money ($)** — the classic balance. Earned from selling prison blocks, jobs, and quests. Used for prison ranks, team costs, kits, and crate openings.
2. **Ultcoins 🪙** — a custom currency from **FrostyEconomy**. Earned by killing **hostile mobs** and winning [chat games](/community/chat-games/). Spent in the [spawner shop](/economy/spawner-shop/).

## Ultcoin basics

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Currency | Ultcoin(s) 🪙 |
| Starting balance | 100 🪙 |
| Max wallet balance | 1,000,000,000 🪙 |
| Decimals shown | 2 |
| Max bank balance | 10,000,000 🪙 |
| Withdrawal fee | None |

## How to check your balance

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/ultcoins` | Check your ultcoin balance |
| `/balance` or `/bal` | Check your Vault balance |
| `/bank` | Open the bank (deposit/withdraw) |
| `/baltop` | View the top 10 richest players |

## Main money sources

- **Ultcoins:** killing hostile mobs ([Mob Kill Rewards](/economy/mob-rewards/)), [chat games](/community/chat-games/), selling spawners back.
- **Vault $:** [jobs](/economy/jobs/) (12 jobs, up to 3 active), the [AFK Zone](/economy/afk-zone/) ($15/min passive), selling prison blocks ([Sell Prices](/prison/sell-prices/)), prison crates, daily rewards, quest rewards.

## Where Vault money is spent

- The **[Server Shop](/economy/server-shop/)** — buy building materials with `/shop` or `/market` (900+ items, sell at 25% of buy).
- **[Crates](/crates/overview/)** — 4,000 to 16,000 Vault per opening.

## Where ultcoins are spent

- The **[Spawner Shop](/economy/spawner-shop/)** — the main ultcoin sink. Buy hostile mob spawners for farms.
- The **[Quest Shop](/economy/quest-shop/)** — spend quest points (earned from daily quests) on enchanted books and a beacon.

## Profiles

Every player has a **profile** you can view — check others' balances and stats with the profiles menu (`/profile`). Profiles are fully viewable for other players.
