The **spawner shop** is the main ultcoin sink. It sells **hostile mob spawners only** — you buy a spawner, place it on your claim, and farm the mobs for ultcoins. Open it with `/ultshop`.

## Selling back

- Spawners are the **only sellable items** in the economy.
- You get back **50%** of what you paid (only for shop-bought spawners).
- Prices get harder in higher tiers.

## Strategy tips

- **Start small:** a Spider or Zombie spawner (~25–35K) pays for itself quickly.
- **Best value:** Blaze and Enderman are popular mid-game farms.
- **Warden isn't in the shop** — but the Elder Guardian (50–100 🪙/kill) and Evoker are strong investments.
- Always place spawners on your [claimed land](/teams/land-claim/) so no one steals the drops!
