While mining, **prison bosses** can spawn around the mines. Defeating them gives big money and EXP rewards.

## Spawning

- Up to **3** bosses can be active at once.
- They spawn every ~**1,000 blocks mined** (spawn chance increases with the Boss Lure pickaxe upgrade).
- Bosses are announced in chat when they appear.

## The bosses

| Boss | Entity | Health | Money reward | EXP reward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Prison Golem** | Iron Golem | 1,000 | $50,000 | 500 |
| **Mine Guardian** | Elder Guardian | 800 | $75,000 | 750 |
| **Void Spectre** | Wither | 1,024 | $100,000 | 1,000 |
| **Warden Prisoner** | Warden | 1,024 | $200,000 | 2,000 |

## Boss tips

- The **Warden Prisoner** is the biggest payout — grab a team or strong gear.
- The **Boss Lure** pickaxe upgrade (prestige 7) makes bosses spawn more often.
- Boss kills also count toward [daily quests](/community/daily-quests/) like "kill X hostile mobs".
