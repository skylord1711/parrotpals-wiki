---
title: Daily Rewards
description: What you can earn from daily login rewards on Parrot Pals.
---

Log in every day to collect daily rewards. The amounts are random within these ranges.

| Reward | Amount range |
| --- | --- |
| Money | $1,000 – $5,000 |
| Tokens | 500 – 1,000 |
| Gems | 1 – 3 |

## Notes

- Rewards refresh on a **weekly cycle**.
- Gems are a premium-style currency used for special purchases.
- Claim your daily reward via the prison/daily reward menu each day — don't let your streak lapse.

Combine daily rewards with [prison crates](/prison/prison-crates/) and [chat games](/community/chat-games/) for a steady income.
