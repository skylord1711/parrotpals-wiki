Every rank has its own mine in the Prison world. You can only mine in your current mine — as you rank up, you move to richer and richer ores.

## How mines work

- **Only your rank's mine** is mineable until you rank up.
- Mines **reset automatically** at 50% mined, so you never run dry.
- Each mine has a spawn pad and a bedrock border with particle effects.
- Prestige tiers have their own **prestige mines** (Prestige I and II and beyond).

## Mining tips

- Always keep your pickaxe upgraded — see [Custom Pickaxes](/prison/pickaxes/).
- Sell blocks as you go (`/sell`) or use autosell.
- Higher-value ores (diamonds, emeralds, netherite) sell for much more — check [Sell Prices](/prison/sell-prices/).
- Prison bosses can spawn while mining — see [Bosses](/prison/bosses/).
