These are the Prison world's own crates, earned from playing (prestige rewards, daily rewards, lucky mining) and opened in the prison menu.

## Common Crate

| Reward | Chance |
| --- | --- |
| 100 Tokens | 75% |
| $500 | 25% |

## Uncommon Crate

| Reward | Chance |
| --- | --- |
| 500 Tokens | 60% |
| $2,500 | 40% |

## Rare Crate

| Reward | Chance |
| --- | --- |
| 2,000 Tokens | 50% |
| $10,000 | 45% |
| **JACKPOT: 5,000 Tokens** | 5% |

## How to get them

- **Rare crate keys** are a prestige reward ([Prestige](/prison/prestige/)).
- Keys can also drop from **Lucky Miner** pickaxe upgrades and rare finds while mining.
- Daily rewards can grant gems/money/tokens — see [Daily Rewards](/prison/daily-rewards/).

These crates are separate from the main **ExcellentCrates** (End, Vote, Animal, Hostile Mob, Nametag) — see [Crates](/crates/overview/).
