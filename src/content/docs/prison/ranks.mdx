## Tips for ranking up

- Mine the ore that sells highest in your current mine (check [Sell Prices](/prison/sell-prices/)).
- Upgrade your [pickaxe](/prison/pickaxes/) — Fortune and Efficiency pay for themselves fast.
- Open [prison crates](/prison/prison-crates/) for instant money and tokens.
- Remember to **sell** regularly (`/sell`) or enable **autosell** while mining.
