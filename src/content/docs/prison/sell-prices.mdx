## What sells best?

Per-block, **Netherite Block ($10,800)** is by far the most valuable, which is why the late-game mines are full of them. But early on, coal blocks and iron pay the best for their stage.

## Pro tip

Turn mined raw ore into **blocks** when you can — e.g. 9 raw iron = 1 raw iron block ($900 vs 9 × $100 = $900, same value) — but ingots sell for more per ingot than ore blocks for some items. When in doubt, sell **ingots** over raw, and **blocks** over ingots for gold (block $1,600 vs 9 × $180 = $1,620 — keep the ingots for gold!).
