The Prison world is the main progression grind on Parrot Pals. You mine, sell your blocks for **money**, and use that money to buy the next **rank**. Higher ranks unlock richer [mines](/prison/mines/) and, eventually, **prestige**.

## The loop

1. **Mine** blocks in your rank's mine.
2. **Sell** your mined blocks for money (`/sell` or automatic).
3. **Rank up** when you have enough money (`/rankup`).
4. **Prestige** at max rank to reset ranks and earn perks ([Prestige](/prison/prestige/)).
5. Repeat — each prestige tier unlocks better [pickaxe upgrades](/prison/pickaxes/) and cosmetics.

## Currencies in prison

- **Money ($)** — Vault balance. Earned by selling blocks and from [prison crates](/prison/prison-crates/). Spent on rank-ups.
- **Tokens** — earned from prison crates, prestige rewards, and lucky mining. Used for prestige and special purchases.
