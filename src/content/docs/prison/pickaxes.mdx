title: Custom Pickaxes
description: Special pickaxes with abilities, upgrade paths, skins, and cosmetics.
---

Mining in Parrot Pals is powered by **custom pickaxes**. You can buy pickaxes with unique abilities, upgrade them heavily, and even reskin them.

## Pickaxe abilities

| Pickaxe | Material | Ability | Enchants |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Speedy Pickaxe** | Golden | Permanent Haste II | Efficiency V, Unbreaking III |
| **Heavy Pickaxe** | Netherite | **2x Tokens** | Efficiency VI, Fortune III, Unbreaking V |
| **Lucky Pickaxe** | Diamond | Extra Lucky Miner (rare drops & keys) | Efficiency V, Fortune V |

## Upgrading your pickaxe

Every pickaxe stat can be upgraded for money. Upgrades get more expensive each level (cost multiplier applies).

| Upgrade | Max level | Base cost | Cost multiplier | Prestige required |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Efficiency | 100 | $1,000 | 1.1x | 0 |
| Fortune | 50 | $1,500 | 1.2x | 0 |
| Haste | 3 | $5,000 | 2.0x | 0 |
| Unbreaking | 100 | $500 | 1.05x | 0 |
| Lucky Miner | 25 | $5,000 | 1.5x | 2 |
| Explosive | 20 | $10,000 | 1.8x | 3 |
| Laser | 10 | $25,000 | 2.5x | 5 |
| Boss Lure | 10 | $50,000 | 3.0x | 7 |

**Tip:** Upgrade **Explosive** and **Laser** as soon as you prestige — they dramatically speed up mining.
