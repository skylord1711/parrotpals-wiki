PvP is **off by default** on Parrot Pals. You choose when to fight.

## How it works

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Default PvP status | **OFF** |
| Toggle command | `/pvp` |
| Combat tag duration | 10 seconds |

- Run `/pvp` to enable PvP — you'll be able to hit other players who also have PvP on.
- When you hit someone in combat, you get a **10-second combat tag**.
- Some worlds can override the setting (e.g. a dedicated PvP area where it's always on).

## PvP tips

- Don't attack players who haven't toggled PvP — you can't, and neither can they.
- In claims, PvP can be disabled by the claim owner — check the [Land Claiming](/teams/land-claim/) page.
- Use the PvP world/arena for fair fights if it's enabled.
