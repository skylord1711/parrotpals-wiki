Teams (via **TeamCore**) let you group up with friends, share a bank, and manage ranks. Open the team menu with `/team`.

## Joining a team

There are **two ways** in — and both need someone to accept on the other side. You can't just hop into a team.

**1. Get invited (team picks you)**

1. A member with the invite permission uses `/team invite <player>`.
2. You get a chat message — click **ACCEPT** or type `/team accept <player>`.
3. You're in. No approval needed beyond accepting the invite.

**2. Browse & request to join (you pick a team)**

1. Open the **[Team Browser](/teams/teams/)** with `/team browser` — it lists every team on the server.
2. Click a team to view it and send a **join request**.
3. The team leader sees your request in their Join Requests menu (`/team requests`) and must **accept or deny** it.
4. You're told when it's approved (`member-joined`); if it's denied you get a "join request was denied" message.

> Note: teams can turn off join requests, so some teams will show as **not accepting requests**. If you get "This team is not currently accepting join requests", they only take invites.

There's no `/team join <team>` that drops you straight in — every team join goes through an **invite** or an **accepted request**.

## Creating a team

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Create cost | $25,000 |
| Max members | 10 |
| Team name length | 3–24 characters |
| Team tag length | 2–8 characters |
| Max description length | 100 characters |

## Ranks

Every team starts with two ranks:

| Rank | Color | Permissions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Member** | Gray | Invite, deposit to bank |
| **Staff** | Aqua | Invite, kick, promote, demote, bank deposit/withdraw, view settings/logs |

- Up to **15 ranks** per team.
- The **Leader** rank is always gold, always kept, and can never be deleted.

## Banned names

Team names/tags can't contain staff terms: `admin`, `moderator`, `staff`, `server`, `owner`, `adm`, `mod`, `op`.

## Team bank

Teams share a bank with a **$** (Vault) balance. Members deposit; Staff+ can withdraw. Use it to save up for big group goals like rank-ups or land.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/team` | Open the team menu |
| `/team browser` | **Browse all teams and send a join request** |
| `/team info [player]` | View a team's info (leader, members, balance) |
| `/team requests` | View pending join requests (Leader/Staff) |
| `/team create <name> <tag>` | Create a team |
| `/team invite <player>` | Invite a player |
| `/team accept <player>` | Accept an incoming invite |
| `/team deny <player>` | Deny an incoming invite |
| `/team leave` | Leave your current team |
| `/team kick <player>` | Kick a player (Staff+) |
| `/team transfer <player>` | Transfer leadership to a member |
| `/team bank` | Open the team bank |
| `/team color` | Change your team color |
| `/team tag` | Change your team tag |
| `/team settings` | Team settings (including accepting requests) |
| `/team chat <message>` | Send a message to your team |
