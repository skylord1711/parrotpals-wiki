Teams (via **TeamCore**) let you group up with friends, share a bank, and manage ranks. Open the team menu with `/team`.

## Joining a team

There are **two ways** in — and both need someone to accept on the other side. You can't just hop into a team.

**1. Get invited (team picks you)**

1. A member with the invite permission uses `/team invite <player>`.
2. You get a chat message — click **ACCEPT** or type `/team accept <player>`.
3. You're in. No approval needed beyond accepting the invite.

**2. Browse & request to join (you pick a team)**

1. Open the **[Team Browser](/teams/teams/)** with `/team browser` — a paginated list of **every team** on the server, showing each team's tag, leader, member count, and description.
2. Click a team to send a **join request** (or get the "This team is not currently accepting join requests" error if they turned requests off).
3. The team reviews it in their **Join Requests** menu (`/team requests`) and **accepts or denies** it.
4. You're told when it's approved; if it's denied you get a "join request was denied" message.

> **Accepting requests is ON by default** for every new team. Staff+ can toggle it off any time in `/team settings` → **Accepting Requests**. Teams with requests off only take invites.

There's no `/team join <team>` that drops you straight in — every team join goes through an **invite** or an **accepted request**.

## The team menu (`/team`)

`/team` opens the main team menu with one-click access to everything:

| Menu button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Members** | View members — left-click to manage their rank, right-click to kick |
| **Ranks** | View and manage your team's ranks |
| **Bank** | Open the team bank |
| **Settings** | Team name, tag, description, color, leader rank name, **Accepting Requests** toggle |
| **Logs** | View team activity logs |
| **Join Requests** | Accept/deny players who want to join |
| **Invite Players** | Invite online players |
| **Team Browser** | Browse public teams and send join requests |
| **Leave Team** | Leave your current team |
| **Create Team** | Create a new team ($25,000) |

### Handling join requests

The **Join Requests** menu (`/team requests`) lists every player asking to join. Anyone with the **invite** permission — or the leader — can open it (the default **Member** rank has invite, so regular members can approve too):

- **Left-click a player → ACCEPT** them into the team.
- **Right-click a player → DENY** them.

## Ranks

Every team starts with two ranks:

| Rank | Color | Permissions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Member** | Gray | Invite, deposit to bank |
| **Staff** | Aqua | Invite, kick, promote, demote, bank deposit/withdraw, view settings/logs |

- Up to **15 ranks** per team.
- The **Leader** rank is always gold, always kept, and can never be deleted.

## Banned names

Team names/tags can't contain staff terms: `admin`, `moderator`, `staff`, `server`, `owner`, `adm`, `mod`, `op`.

## Team bank

Teams share a bank with a **$** (Vault) balance. Members deposit; Staff+ can withdraw. Use it to save up for big group goals like rank-ups or land.

| `/team deny <player>` | Deny an incoming invite |

| `/team kick <player>` | Kick a player (Staff+) |
| `/team transfer <player>` | Transfer leadership to a member |
| `/team bank` | Open the team bank |
| `/team color` | Change your team color |
| `/team tag` | Change your team tag |
| `/team settings` | Team settings (including accepting requests) |
