Protect your builds with **land claims** (via **UltimateLandClaim**). Claims use a **Golden Shovel** and claim blocks.

## How to claim

1. Get a **Golden Shovel**.
2. **Right-click** one corner of your area.
3. **Right-click** the opposite corner.
4. Your claim is created (minimum size 25 blocks, maximum 25,000 blocks).

## Claim blocks

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Starting claim blocks | 100 |
| Earned per minute of playtime | 5 |
| Max claim blocks | 50,000 |
| Bonus blocks (permission) | `ultimatelandclaim.bonus.<amount>` |

You earn claim blocks just by playing — 5 per minute up to 50,000 total.

## Protection

Claims protect your builds from griefing:
- **PvP** can be turned off inside claims (configurable).
- Other players can't break blocks in your claim.
- Spawn protection is enabled around spawn.

## World rules

- Claiming is allowed in all worlds by default (no whitelist/blacklist).
- Claiming near spawn is restricted.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/claim` or `/ultimatelandclaim` | Open the claim menu |
| `/claim pos1` / `/claim pos2` | Set corners with commands |
| `/unclaim` | Remove a claim |
| `/claim trust <player>` | Give a player access |
| `/claims` | View your claims |

## Tips

- Always claim your **spawner farms** so nobody steals the drops!
- Trust your team members (`/claim trust`) so they can build and use the farm.
- Playtime accrual means claim blocks stack up over time — max 50,000.
