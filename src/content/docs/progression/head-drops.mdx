---
title: Head Drops
description: Which mobs drop their heads on Parrot Pals, and how rare each drop is.
---

Most mobs on Parrot Pals drop their head when you kill them, powered by the **HeadDrop** plugin. Heads are collectible trophies — every one keeps a **lore tag** showing who killed it, on what date, and with which weapon.

## How it works

- Every kill rolls the mob's **drop chance** — kill enough of a mob and the head will eventually drop.
- **Player heads** drop when you kill another player in PvP.
- Passive animals (cows, chickens, etc.) drop heads too, just far less often than hostiles.
- Dropped heads despawn after **5 minutes** if nobody picks them up.

## Drop chances by rarity

| Rarity | Chance | Mobs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Always** | 100% | Player heads (PvP only), Ender Dragon, Wither, Elder Guardian |
| **Rare** | 50% | Wither Skeleton, Blaze, Ghast, Shulker, Warden, Ravager, Evoker, Vindicator, Illusioner, Vex, Pillager, Giant, Breeze + all newer mobs |
| **Common hostile** | 30% | Zombie, Skeleton, Spider, Cave Spider, Creeper, Drowned, Husk, Stray, Bogged, Enderman, Endermite, Phantom, Slime, Magma Cube, Witch, Guardian, Hoglin, Zoglin, Piglin, Piglin Brute, Zombified Piglin, Zombie Villager |
| **Passive & wild** | 20% | Farm animals (Cow, Pig, Sheep, Chicken, Rabbit, Mooshroom, Goat), Villagers, Iron & Snow Golems, Wolves, Cats, Foxes, Axolotls, Dolphins, Turtles, Frogs, Fish, Squid, Bats, and other peaceful mobs |

## Tips

- Heads make great **decorations** for bases — line them up on item frames for a trophy wall.
- **Rare mobs** are worth farming if you want the flashiest collection: wither skeletons, raid mobs, and nether mobs pay off fastest.
- Boss heads (Wither, Ender Dragon, Elder Guardian) **always drop** — don't miss them.
