## How to get prefixes

- Most prefixes come from the **Nametag Crate** (mythic, rare, and common drops).
- Every player has the **Default** (✦Parrot Member✦) prefix.
- **FrostyKing** is the special streamer/owner prefix.
- Some community prefixes (JoeysFan, FatGoldFish, BittenGrassTip...) reference community members.

## How to equip

1. Run `/prefix` to open the prefix menu.
2. Click the prefix you want to equip.
3. It shows in chat immediately.
