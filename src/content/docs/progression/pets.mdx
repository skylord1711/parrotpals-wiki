Pets are cosmetic companions that follow you around, powered by **FrostyPets** with **PetBlocks** templates. There are **34 pets** to collect — including a full flock of animals from parrots and dolphins to pandas and axolotls, plus a set of custom-designed head pets.

## How to get a pet

- Pets come from **pet coupons** — right-click the coupon to redeem the pet.
- Admins can grant pets directly with `/fpets give <player> <pet>` or hand out coupons with `/fpets givecoupon <pet> <player>`.
- Open your pets menu (`/fpets`) to select, equip, call, ride, or wear a pet as a hat.

## Commands

| Command | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `/fpets` | Open the pets menu. |
| `/fpets my` | List the pets you own. |
| `/fpets equip <pet>` | Equip (spawn) a pet you own. |
| `/fpets call` | Call your equipped pet. |
| `/fpets ride` | Ride your equipped pet. |
| `/fpets hat` | Wear your equipped pet as a hat. |

## Notes

- Pets are cosmetic — they don't affect gameplay.
- Each pet has a matching **coupon** (a spawn egg for mob pets, a custom head for head pets) — redeem it to add the pet to your collection.
- Customize your pet's look (size, colors, and more) from the pets menu.
- Pet templates are also used for the prestige pets, so grind those prestiges!
