## Gameplay style

Parrot Pals is a **SMP + Prison hybrid**. There's a full survival world (SMP) for building, exploring, and settling with teams and land claims — plus a dedicated **Prison** world where you grind ranks, prestige, custom pickaxes, and tokens.

## Worlds

| World | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `Prison` | Ranked mining mines, lobby, prison bosses, daily rewards |
| SMP / survival world | Build, claim land, farm spawners, hunt mobs for ultcoins |
| PvP area | Optional PvP, off by default |

There is **no End dimension**. The **End Crate** and **Vote Crate** are how you earn End loot (Elytra, Dragon Egg, etc.) — see [Crates](/crates/overview/). For open-world survival, use `/rtp` to teleport into **the Wild** beyond spawn.

## World border

The world border sits at **50,000 blocks** in every direction — from **−50,000 to +50,000** on both X and Z (a 100,000-block square centered on `0,0`). It runs the full height of the world, from **bedrock** all the way up to the **build limit**, so you have the entire vertical range to work with. You can't go past the border, but there's plenty of room to build, claim, and explore before you ever hit it.

These are separate from the cosmetic **prefixes** you equip yourself (e.g. `✦Parrot Member✦` or `✦ILoveFrosty✦`) — see [Prefixes](/progression/prefixes/).

## Policies & tips

- **PvP** is **off by default** — you choose when to fight with `/pvp`.
- **Griefing** is prevented via land claims — claim your builds with a **Golden Shovel**.
- **World border** is 50,000 blocks in each direction (±50k on X and Z), full height from bedrock to build limit.
- **Mob kill money**: only *hostile* mobs give ultcoins. Animals give nothing.
- Keep an eye on chat — [chat games](/community/chat-games/) pay out ultcoins throughout the day.
